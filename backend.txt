1. PROJECT
============================================================

Project Name:
DecentraWill

Purpose:

DecentraWill is a secure digital trust, verification, asset-management,
governance, and inheritance-coordination platform built around the
traditional will-making process.

IMPORTANT:

The traditional legal will remains the underlying legal artifact.

This system DOES NOT:

- replace courts
- replace probate
- replace advocates/lawyers
- replace legally required witnesses
- automatically determine death
- automatically transfer legally regulated physical assets
- claim that blockchain overrides applicable law
- store raw wills or sensitive PII on a public blockchain
- provide unsupervised legal advice

19. VERSIONING
============================================================

Every material modification creates a new version.

Example:

V1 DRAFT
   ↓
REVIEW
   ↓
FINALIZED
   ↓
ACTIVE

Material change:

V2 DRAFT
   ↓
FINALIZED
   ↓
ACTIVE

V1 → SUPERSEDED

Rules:

- finalized versions cannot be edited
- finalized versions cannot be deleted
- historical versions remain preserved
- each version has its own hash
- each version has verification evidence
- attestation may need to be repeated
- only one ACTIVE version per estate

Database constraint:

UNIQUE(estate_id) WHERE status = ACTIVE

Also:

UNIQUE(estate_id, version_no)
